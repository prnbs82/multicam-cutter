How to use this tool

1 · Edit words & clips: press ✨ Tighten automatically, then select any words you don't want and press Del. Select the words of a clip and press ▣.
2 · Cameras: pick the clip; angles are pre-filled from where the speaker looks; press 1/2/3 while it plays to change them; Z reframes a shot.
3 · Images: ✓ Use the suggested pictures you like, or type what to look for.
Export from any tab. Every cut is made to look natural by the app; tick Advanced in the header to see and tune everything.
